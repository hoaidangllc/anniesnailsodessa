Privacy Policy

Effective: September 27, 2026

Annie's Nails & Spa is operated by ANH HOAI NAILS & SPA LLC in Odessa, Texas. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be collected, used, and protected when you visit our website, contact the salon, book services, participate in rewards, or communicate with us.

Personal Data We Collect

Depending on how you interact with us, information may include your name, phone number, email address, appointment or service information, communication preferences, rewards information, and information you voluntarily provide to us. Our online appointment booking is provided by a third-party booking service, which may collect information under its own privacy practices.

How We Use Personal Data

We may use information to provide salon services, manage appointments, respond to customer questions, support rewards and customer service, send service-related communications, and, where permitted and with appropriate consent, send promotional or marketing communications.

SMS and Mobile Information

We do not sell, rent, or share mobile numbers or SMS opt-in consent with third parties or affiliates for their own marketing or promotional purposes. Any personal information that we collect, as identified in the “Personal Data We Collect” section, will not be shared with third parties for their own marketing purposes.

We may use service providers only as necessary to operate our website, appointment, communications, messaging, analytics, or other business services. Those providers may process information on our behalf to provide those services and are not authorized by us to use SMS consent for their own marketing.

Text Messaging Choices

If you have opted in to receive text messages from Annie's Nails & Spa, message frequency may vary. Message and data rates may apply. You may opt out at any time by replying STOP. For help, reply HELP. Opting out of promotional texts does not prevent necessary non-marketing communications where otherwise permitted.

Cookies and Analytics

Our website may use cookies and analytics technologies, including Google Analytics, to understand website usage and improve the customer experience. These technologies may collect device, browser, and general usage information.

Data Security and Retention

We use reasonable administrative and technical measures to protect information. No online system can be guaranteed completely secure. We retain information only as reasonably necessary for business, legal, customer-service, and compliance purposes.

Your Choices

You may contact us to ask about your information or communication preferences. You may also use the opt-out instructions included in marketing messages.

Third-Party Services and Links

Our website links to third-party services such as our appointment booking provider, maps, review platforms, and social networks. Their privacy practices are governed by their own policies.
