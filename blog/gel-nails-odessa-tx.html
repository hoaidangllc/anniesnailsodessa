GEL MANICURE GUIDE

Gel nails: the practical guide before you book.

Gel is popular because it cures quickly and keeps a glossy finish longer than traditional polish—but it is not the same thing as acrylic, dip or builder gel.

What gel polish is good at

Gloss

Gel keeps a polished, high-shine finish without waiting for traditional polish to air-dry.

Everyday wear

A good fit when you want natural-nail color that can handle normal daily life.

Simple maintenance

Ideal for guests who want color without adding the structure or length of acrylic.

How long does gel last?

Wear varies with nail condition, growth, application and what your hands go through every day. Many clients plan their next visit around visible growth or when the finish no longer looks fresh rather than waiting for the product to fail completely.

Gel vs dip vs acrylic

Gel polish is primarily a color system on the natural nail. Dip adds more strength and thickness. Acrylic is the better-known option when you want substantial structure, extensions or dramatic length. If your priority is simply glossy color on your natural nails, gel is often the cleanest starting point.

Removal matters

Do not peel cured gel off. Peeling can take layers of the natural nail with it. Proper removal takes more patience but is the better choice for keeping the nail plate in good condition between services.

French or solid color?

Solid color is simple and versatile. French gives a more defined tip and requires extra detail, which is why it is priced differently on Annie's menu. Bring a photo if you have a particular French thickness, shape or color combination in mind.
