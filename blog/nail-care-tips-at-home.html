BETWEEN APPOINTMENTS

Small habits that help your nails look better for longer.

You do not need a complicated home routine. The biggest wins are usually moisture, protection and resisting the urge to pick at product.

1

Use cuticle oil consistently

A small amount around the cuticle and surrounding skin helps the manicure look cared for as the nail grows out.

2

Wear gloves for cleaning

Long water exposure and household cleaners are rough on hands and manicures. Gloves are an easy layer of protection.

3

Do not use your nails as tools

Opening cans, scraping labels and prying packages puts leverage on the free edge and on enhancement products.

4

File a snag instead of pulling it

If a natural nail edge catches, gently smooth the snag rather than tearing it farther. For enhancement lifting or a larger break, a salon repair is the cleaner option.

5

Do not peel off gel, dip or acrylic

Picking product off can remove surface layers from the natural nail. Plan proper removal when you are ready for a change.

6

Book maintenance before everything is overgrown

Extensions and structured services change balance as the natural nail grows. A regular maintenance rhythm usually looks cleaner than waiting until multiple nails need repair.

The routine can stay simple: cuticle oil, hand lotion, gloves for wet work, gentle treatment and proper removal. Expensive home products are not a requirement for basic maintenance.

What if you want to change services?

If you currently wear acrylic but want dip, or gel but want a natural-nail break, mention the existing product when you book. Removal can change appointment time and price. Annie's menu lists Gel Remove with Service at $10 and Acrylic Remove Only at $10.
