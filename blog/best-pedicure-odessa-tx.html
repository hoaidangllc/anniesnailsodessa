Regular polish or gel?

Regular polish is included with the pedicure menu. If you want longer-wearing color, Shellac Gel is a $20 add-on; French Shellac is $25. If you prefer classic French with regular polish, the add-on is $5.

How often should you book?

There is no single schedule for everyone. Many people like a roughly three-to-four-week rhythm for appearance and maintenance, while others book around events, travel or sandal season. If you have pain, swelling, infection, open wounds or a medical foot concern, a nail salon is not a substitute for medical care.

What should you expect from a good pedicure?

You should know what service you selected, what is included and what any add-ons cost. The work area should look cared for, and you should feel comfortable asking questions. A relaxing service is better when there are no surprises at checkout.

Before you arrive

Bring inspiration if you want a specific color or French finish. If you are removing existing gel or have a special request, mention it when booking or when you arrive so the service can be planned correctly.
