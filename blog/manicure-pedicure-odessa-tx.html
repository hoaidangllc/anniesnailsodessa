SERVICE GUIDE

Manicure, pedicure—or both?

If you do not visit nail salons often, the menu can look more complicated than it needs to. Start with what you want done, then choose the finish.

Manicure

Focuses on hands and fingernails. At Annie's, Gel Color Manicure is $35 and Gel French Manicure is $40.

Pedicure

Focuses on feet and toenails. Basic Pedicure starts at $30, with multiple upgraded spa options.

If you want color on natural fingernails

Gel is a straightforward option for glossy, cured color. Dip is another choice when you prefer a thicker, stronger-feeling powder system. Annie's menu lists Dip Color at $40+ and Dip French at $45+.

If you want length or more structure

Acrylic is usually the more relevant category. Annie's Full Set Shellac starts at $50+, with Pink & White at $60+. Length, shape and design can change the final price.

If you mainly want a foot-care visit

Choose a pedicure tier based on how much spa time you want. The Basic service covers the essentials; Deluxe, Luxury and premium tiers add different spa experiences. Gel and French finishes are optional add-ons.

Can you book hands and feet together?

Yes—many guests combine services when preparing for travel, events or simply to get everything done in one salon visit. Allow more time than you would for a single service.

What to bring to your appointment

If you have a specific color, shape or design in mind, a reference photo is useful. If you have existing product that needs removal, mention it so there is enough time and you understand any removal charge.

Before booking: Annie's public menu shows starting prices and add-ons so you can narrow down the service first. For a custom design or unusual request, call (432) 299-9999.
