FIRST-VISIT CHECKLIST

How to choose a nail salon without relying on hype.

A salon does not have to be the fanciest one online. It has to fit your service, style, budget and expectations in real life.

1

Know what service you want

Gel, dip, acrylic and pedicure are different services. Compare salons based on the work you actually plan to book.

2

Look for visible pricing

Starting prices should be easy to find, with a clear note that length, shape, art, repairs or removal can change the final price.

3

Check recent photos

Look for the shapes and finishes you personally like. A salon can have great work that simply is not your style.

4

Read review patterns

Pay attention to repeated themes—service, wait time, friendliness, durability and problem handling—rather than one extreme review.

5

Notice cleanliness when you arrive

The work area, pedicure area and general organization should make you feel comfortable.

6

Ask before adding extras

French tips, designs, longer lengths, special shapes and removal can affect price. Clarify before the work starts.

7

Test the booking experience

A good website, clear phone number, directions and simple booking reduce friction before you ever sit down.

8

Judge the first visit for yourself

The most useful question is whether you would comfortably return. Your own experience matters more than a marketing label.
