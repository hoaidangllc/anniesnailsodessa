SALON VISIT CHECKLIST

Cleanliness is something you should be able to notice.

You do not need to be a salon professional to pay attention to the basics. A few visible details can help you feel more confident about where you receive services.

✓

Look at the station, not just the lobby

A beautiful front desk does not tell you how the actual service area is maintained. Notice whether the station is reset and organized for the next guest.

✓

Pay attention to tools and single-use items

Different tools and supplies are handled differently. If you are unsure whether something is fresh or prepared for your service, it is okay to ask.

✓

Notice the pedicure area

The chair, basin area and nearby work surfaces should look cared for between guests. A busy salon can still have an orderly service process.

✓

Speak up if something makes you uncomfortable

You are allowed to ask a question before a service starts. A clear answer is better than sitting through an appointment feeling unsure.

✓

Clean does not mean “smells strongly like cleaner”

Appearance, organization and handling practices are more useful observations than fragrance alone.

Why this article is different: cleanliness should be part of choosing any salon, not a marketing claim used to declare one salon “the best.” Use the same checklist wherever you go.
